General Paper Formatting

Core Layout

MLA 9th edition emphasizes clarity, consistency, and standard layout conventions across academic disciplines.

Margins & Font

1-inch margins on all sides. Use standard fonts like Times New Roman 12pt, Georgia, or Computer Modern.

Spacing & Indentation

Double-space the entire document, including block quotes and Works Cited. Indent paragraphs 0.5 inches.

Header & Heading

Right-aligned running head with your last name and page number (e.g., Cabrera 1).

First-Page Block

Top left: Your Name, Instructor, Course, and Date (Day Month Year format: 28 Aug 2026).

In-Text Parenthetical Citations

Author-Page System

In MLA style, in-text citations direct readers directly to the corresponding entry in the Works Cited list.

Standard Paraphrase / Direct Quote

"Academic integrity requires active commitment and ethical stewardship."

(Cabrera 45)
With Signal Phrase

According to Cabrera, clarity in structure enhances scholarly dialogue (45).

Note: Page number remains in parentheses at the end of the clause.

The 9 Core Elements System

MLA 9 Container Model

MLA 9th edition uses a flexible container system built from nine core elements, listed in exact order with prescribed punctuation.

  1. 1. Author. Period
  2. 2. Title of Source. Period
  3. 3. Title of Container, Comma
  4. 4. Contributor, Comma
  5. 5. Version, Comma
  6. 6. Number, Comma
  7. 7. Publisher, Comma
  8. 8. Publication Date, Comma
  9. 9. Location. Period

Citing Poetry in MLA 9

Verse Rules
Works Cited Entry
Frost, Robert. "The Road Not Taken." Mountain Interval, Henry Holt and Company, 1916, p. 9.

In-Text Citations: Cite by line numbers, not page numbers, if line numbers are provided in the text.

  • First citation: include the word "lines" → (Frost, lines 1–4)
  • Subsequent citations: use numbers only → (Frost 12–15)
  • Line breaks in inline quotes: use a forward slash with spaces → "Two roads diverged in a yellow wood, / And sorry I could not travel both" (Frost 1–2).

Lesson: Excellence in scholarly work reflects diligence and respect in all efforts.

How to Cite Scripture in MLA 9th Edition

  • In the Works Cited list, cite the specific edition of the Bible used (e.g., The Holy Bible, English Standard Version. Crossway Bibles, 2016).
  • In parenthetical citations, include the title of the Bible edition (italicized) in the first citation, followed by book, chapter, and verse: (English Standard Version, Prov. 12.22).
  • Subsequent citations can omit the Bible title if the edition remains unchanged: (Col. 3.23).

How to Apply This in Your MLA Paper

1. Short Quote Integrated in Paragraph

In his assignment reflection, the speaker emphasizes his identity by stating, "I am twenty-two, colored, born in Winston-Salem. / I went to school there, then Durham, raised there" (Hughes, lines 7–9). This detail establishes his unique perspective within the classroom.

Note: Use a forward slash / with spaces on both sides to show line breaks in poetry.

2. Block Quote Format (3+ Lines of Verse)

It's not easy to know what is true for you or me

at twenty-two, my age. But I guess I'm what

I feel and see and hear, Harlem, I hear you: (Hughes 16–18)

Note: Indent block quotes 0.5 inches from the left margin, omit quotation marks, and place the citation after the punctuation.

3. Works Cited Page Layout (Hanging Indent)

Works Cited

Hughes, Langston. "Theme for English B." The Collected Poems of Langston Hughes, edited by Arnold Rampersad and David Roessel, Vintage Books, 1994, pp. 247–248.

Note: Title "Works Cited" is centered at the top. The entry uses a hanging indent (0.5 inches on subsequent lines).

Paper Structure & Formatting

Complete guide to MLA 9th edition paper layout and special elements.

Format Guide

First-Page Heading (No Title Page Required)

MLA 9th edition does not require a separate title page. Instead, format the top-left corner of your first page:

Justin Cabrera

Dr. Smith

ENG 102

28 Aug. 2026

Formatting: Single-space, left-aligned, 1-inch margins. Double-space begins after this heading and the centered title.

Title on First Page

After the heading, center your essay title on its own line. Do not underline, italicize, or use quotation marks (unless the title contains a quoted phrase).

The Power of Language in "Theme for English B"

Note: Your essay body begins on the next line, double-spaced.

Running Header (Every Page)

Right-align your last name and page number at the top of every page, including the Works Cited.

Cabrera 1

Format: 0.5 inches from the top of the page, right-aligned, no extra space.

Table Formatting

  • • Label above: "Table 1. [Title]"
  • • Caption style: Use sentence case, flush left
  • • Border: Simple lines, no shading (unless necessary)
  • • Source: Include below if data is external
  • • Font: Same as body text (10pt minimum)

Figure / Image Formatting

  • • Label below: "Fig. 1. Description of image."
  • • Citation: Include source information
  • • Alignment: Center if standalone, or left if text flows
  • • Captions: Double-spaced, same font as body
  • • Copyright: Ensure permissions noted if necessary

Endnotes & Footnotes (Content Notes)

MLA 9 permits content notes for clarification or supplementary information. Use sparingly.

Superscript Number in Text:

The significance of this metaphor1 cannot be overstated in contemporary discourse.

Endnote (on separate page before Works Cited):

Notes

1 Critics debate whether the "yellow wood" represents nature's indifference or human choice.

Placement: Create a "Notes" page after your essay but before Works Cited. Number all notes to match in-text superscripts.

Final Formatting Checklist

  • ✓ All margins are 1 inch on all sides
  • ✓ Double-spaced throughout (including quotes and Works Cited)
  • ✓ Font is readable (Times New Roman 12pt, Georgia, Computer Modern)
  • ✓ First page has 4-line heading on top left + centered title
  • ✓ Running header on every page (Last Name + page number, right-aligned)
  • ✓ Paragraph indentation is 0.5 inches
  • ✓ Works Cited uses hanging indents (0.5 inches on subsequent lines)
  • ✓ All source entries alphabetized by author's last name
  • ✓ No extra space between entries or paragraphs
